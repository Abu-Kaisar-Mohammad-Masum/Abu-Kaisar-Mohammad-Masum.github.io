👋 About

Abu Kaisar Mohammad Masum is a Ph.D. candidate in Quantum Machine Learning and Emerging Computing at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, where he also earned an M.S. in Computer Science. He received his B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Bangladesh. His research has been published in leading venues, including DAC, AAAI, DATE, ISLPED, IEEE TVLSI, IEEE TBME, Nature Portfolio, IEEE Embedded Systems Letters, ISCAS, QCE, ICCD, ICTAI, GLSVLSI, MWSCAS, AAAI Fall Symposium, ARQTEC@HPCA, and WUC@ASPLOS. In 2025, he was selected as a DAC Young Fellow. Most recently, he was part of the winning team in the NASA Beyond the Algorithm Challenge: Novel Computing Architectures for Flood Analysis, securing a $100K grant for pioneering research integrating Quantum Computing and Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC) for real-world flood analysis.
